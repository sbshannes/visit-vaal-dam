Four towns, one dam

Towns of the Vaal Dam

Vaal Marina, Deneysville, Oranjeville and Frankfort: four very different bases for your Vaal escape.

Gauteng shore

Vaal Marina

A laid-back holiday town on the Gauteng shore, built for people who love the water. Slipways, a yacht club, big sunsets and that unhurried weekend feeling.

Free State shore, near the wall

Deneysville

The biggest town on the dam, named after Deneys Reitz. Yacht clubs, regattas, flying-boat history and easy access to open water.

Oranjeville

A quieter, slower corner of the dam. A good base for fishing, camping and long, lazy days beside the water.

Frankfort

A handsome Free State farming town on the Wilge River, the big tributary that feeds the dam, with sandstone buildings declared national monuments.

The water is waiting. Come and find your Vaal.

Pack the cooler box, load the rods or the wakeboard, and point the car south. Whether it is a sunrise at Vaal Marina, a sail out of Deneysville, a quiet weekend at Oranjeville or a stroll past Frankfort's sandstone monuments, the Vaal Dam is closer than you think.
