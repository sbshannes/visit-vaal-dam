Oranjeville on the Vaal Dam

Where the Vaal Dam slows right down.

Photo: Ossewa, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons. Credits

Oranjeville is a small town on the Free State shore of the Vaal Dam, south-east of Deneysville in the Metsimaholo municipality. It is quieter and more relaxed than its busier neighbours, the kind of place you come to switch off.

Life here revolves around the water. Oranjeville makes a good base for fishing, camping and boating, with long stretches of shoreline, wide views across the dam and big skies at night. Days are measured in fish caught, chapters read and the number of times you put the kettle on.

If you want the dam without the crowds, Oranjeville is worth the extra few kilometres.

Why visit

  • A slower, quieter pace on the Free State shore
  • Fishing and camping close to the water
  • Big night skies away from city lights
  • About two hours from Johannesburg
