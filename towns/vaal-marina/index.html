Vaal Marina on the Vaal Dam

A waterfront holiday town where the weekend starts the moment you see the water.

Photo: Ossewa, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons. Credits

Vaal Marina sits on the Gauteng (northern) shore of the Vaal Dam in the Midvaal Local Municipality, roughly 100 kilometres and about an hour and a half by road from Johannesburg. It began life as a holiday township for city families who wanted a second home on the water, and that is still its heartbeat: boats on trailers in the driveways, slipways down to the water and a slower pace the moment you arrive.

This is a place built around the dam. Marinas and launch sites give easy access for ski boats, yachts and fishing boats, and the local yacht club, Pennant Nine, races on the water here. Anglers work the shoreline for carp and barbel, and on calm mornings the skiers are out early.

More and more people now live here all year round, but on a summer Saturday the weekenders still roll in from Johannesburg and the East Rand. Come for a day of sun and water, a long weekend in a holiday house, or a longer stay to remember what quiet sounds like.

Why visit

  • Boating, sailing and water sports on the northern shore
  • Shore and boat fishing for carp and barbel
  • Big Highveld sunsets over the water
  • One of the closest points on the dam for Johannesburg, the East Rand and the Vaal Triangle
